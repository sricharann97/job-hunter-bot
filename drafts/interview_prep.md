# Interview Prep — Accounting Fresher (working notes)

## Likely questions for Hyderabad accounting fresher roles

1. **Tell me about yourself.** — B.Com from Osmania University, 6th-semester results awaited; Tally ERP 9 with GST certified (GEENI, Grade A); Complete Course for Accountants; MS-Office Diploma. Practical work in journal entries, GST returns (GSTR-1/3B), TDS, invoicing, and Excel reporting.
2. **What is the difference between GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B?** — GSTR-1 lists outward (sales) supplies; GSTR-3B is the monthly self-declared summary return with net liability and tax payment.
3. **How do you reconcile a bank statement in Tally?** — Use Bank Reconciliation in Tally: match bank statement entries with ledger vouchers date-wise; unmatched items show as pending differences.
4. **Explain TDS on professional fees.** — 194J: 10% TDS on professional/technical fees above the threshold; deducted at payment/credit whichever earlier; deposited by 7th of next month, TDS return in 26Q.
5. **What is e-invoicing?** — GST invoice reporting via IRP (invoice registration portal); generates IRN and QR code; mandatory for turnover above ₹5 crore; helps auto-populate GSTR-1.
6. **Rate yourself in Excel.** — VLOOKUP/XLOOKUP, SUMIFS, pivot tables, data validation, conditional formatting; used in assignment reporting.

## STAR-style examples to prepare

- A tally/GST exercise: prepared GSTR-1 for a sample set, reconciled with 3B.
- An Excel dashboard: monthly expense report with pivots.
- A deadline story: handled multiple assignments in exam season.

## Questions to ask the interviewer

- Day-to-day responsibilities of this role? Training/onboarding plan? Software stack (Tally Prime/Zoho)? Growth path to full accountant?
